Civil Engineering Material Estimation Guide

Understand standard thumb rules and calculation formulas used by structural engineers.

Brick Masonry Standard

Standard brick size is assumed to be 9" x 4.5" x 3". For a 9-inch thick brick wall, approximately 13.5 to 14 modular bricks are required per cubic foot (CFT) of wall volume including 10% mortar waste.

Cement Mortar & Plaster Ratio

For brick laying, a 1:5 ratio (1 part cement to 5 parts sand) is standard. For plastering, a 1/2-inch layer of 1:4 cement-sand mix is calculated for both internal and external wall surfaces.

Reinforced Concrete Roof Slab

A standard 5-inch thick RCC roof slab uses M20 concrete mix (1:1.5:3 ratio). Steel reinforcement (Saria) is estimated at 80 kg to 100 kg per cubic meter of concrete slab volume.

Wastage Factor

A standard 5% wastage factor is automatically applied across bricks, sand, and aggregates to prevent shortages on the job site due to breakage and handling loss.

About BuildCalc Pro

BuildCalc Pro is an enterprise micro-SaaS application engineered for civil contractors, quantity surveyors, real estate developers, and homeowners. It eliminates manual spreadsheet calculations by providing instant, accurate material volume breakdowns and budget forecasts.

Built with high-performance responsive web components, zero external heavy dependencies, and multi-currency calculation support.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. BuildCalc Pro operates 100% on client-side browser execution. We do not collect, store, or transmit your structural measurements, cost data, or personal details to any external servers.

All calculations, unit rates, and settings are stored locally on your device session.

Terms of Use

BuildCalc Pro estimations are based on standard civil engineering thumb rules and average material density ratios. Actual material consumption on site may vary depending on local brick dimensions, site topography, structural engineering specifications, and labor efficiency.

Always consult a qualified structural engineer for official blueprint sign-offs before purchasing site inventory.
